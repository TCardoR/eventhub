# Actors and Permissions

## Administrator

The admin (or administrator) has general control over the platform.

***They can:***
- Manage Users
- Manage Companies
- Manage Contacts
- Create and modify events
- Consult reservations
- Register and consult payments
- Consult audit
- Consult quality indicators

## Organizer

The organizer is the one in charge of different events.

***They can:***
- Consult companies and contacts
- Create and modify events
- Register payments
- Consult quality indicators of events.

## Customer

The customer represents a person linked to a company.

***They can:***
- Consult available events
- Create reservations
- Consult their own reservations
- Cancel reservations under certain conditions
- Consult their own payments.
