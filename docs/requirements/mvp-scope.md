# MVP Scope

## Included

- User Management (create, modify, disable, etc)
- Login
- Roles Management
- JWT automation
- Company Management (create, modify, disable, etc)
- Contact Management (create, modify, disable, etc)
- Event Management (create, modify, disable, etc)
- Reservations Management (create, modify, disable, etc)
- Payment Management (create, modify, disable, etc)
- Audit for all objects 
- Dashboard to all objects

## Excluded

- A complete web interfance
- Phone app
- A real payment gateway
- Electronic billing
- Real email manage
- Kafka
- WebFlux
- Kubernetes
- DynamoDB
- Terraform
- Microservices
- A.I recomendations
- Multi-company or multi-tenant system.

## Future Evolution

The excluded information doesn't mean they're being abandoned. It means they won't hold up the first working version.
