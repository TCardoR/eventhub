# Product Vision

## Problem

A lot of companies manage events, clients, contacts, reservations, and payments using separate tools, spreadsheets, or manual processes. This generates duplicate information, reservations without proper monitoring, reconciled payments, incorrect tracking, difficulty knowing availability, and low visibility into event performance.

## Proposed Solution

Eventhub would be a backend platform that will let companies register other clients' companies, manage their contacts, create and publish events, manage reservations, control payments, consult their operation history, and obtain basic quality indicators.

## Product Goal

Centralize the management of businesses, contacts, events, reservations, and payments on a traceable and maintainable platform.

## Initial Product Type

- REST API.
- Modular monolith.
- Single operating organization.
- Backend-first development.

## Success Criteria

- Register companies and contacts.
- Create and publish events.
- Create reservations without exceeding capacity.
- Record payments and check balances.
- Audit significant transactions.
